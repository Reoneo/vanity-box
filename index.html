Vanity.box is a Web3 identity platform that replaces long, complicated wallet addresses with a single easy-to-read name. Our platform supports a wide range of blockchain naming services including ENS domains on Ethereum, Basenames on Base, IOTA names, Aptos names, TON DNS, Sui domains, Unstoppable Domains, and Worldcoin identities. Users can register vanity subdomains, link multiple wallets across chains, and present a unified personalised Web3 identity to the rest of the world.

With Vanity.box you can register a vanity subdomain on World Chain, Base, Ethereum, Aptos, IOTA, TON, and Sui. Each subdomain becomes a memorable human readable identifier that points to your wallet, decentralized profile, social accounts, NFT collections, POAPs, on-chain credentials, and verified claims. The platform integrates with Ethereum Name Service, Basenames, IOTA Identity, Aptos Names, TON Connect, Sui Name Service, Unstoppable Domains, Farcaster, EFP follower graphs, Talent Protocol scores, and Polymarket activity.
