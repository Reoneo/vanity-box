Personalised Web3 Wallet Addresses & Blockchain Identity Names

Replace long crypto wallet addresses with one easy-to-read name

Vanity.box is a Web3 identity platform that replaces long, complicated wallet addresses with a single easy-to-read name. Users can register vanity subdomains, link multiple wallets across chains, and present a unified personalised Web3 identity.

Supported blockchains and naming services

Ethereum, Base, IOTA, Aptos, TON, Sui, World Chain and Unstoppable Domains

Vanity.box supports ENS domains on Ethereum, Basenames on Base, IOTA names, Aptos names, TON DNS, Sui domains, Unstoppable Domains, and Worldcoin identities. Each subdomain becomes a memorable human readable identifier that points to your wallet, decentralized profile, social accounts, NFT collections, POAPs, on-chain credentials, and verified claims.

Features

Cross-chain profiles, gasless onboarding and verified identity

The platform integrates with Ethereum Name Service, Basenames, IOTA Identity, Aptos Names, TON Connect, Sui Name Service, Unstoppable Domains, Farcaster, EFP follower graphs, Talent Protocol scores, and Polymarket activity. Vanity.box improves user experience and interoperability across blockchains with smoother wallet and domain linking, gasless onboarding, subdomains as verified IDs, human priority blockspace, and free gas for unique humans verified by World ID.

Get started with your Web3 identity

Search any name, register your vanity subdomain, link wallets from Ethereum, Base, World Chain, IOTA, Aptos, TON, Sui, and Polygon, and consolidate your entire on-chain reputation under one memorable name.
